OUR ACADEMIC HOME · VISTAS

School of Pharmaceutical Sciences.

A foundation for pharmacy education, research, and service.

Established in 1992 by the Vaels Educational Trust as Vels College of Pharmacy, the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences at VISTAS has grown into a premier institution offering UG, PG, and doctoral programs in Pharmacy. Integrated into VISTAS in 2008, a Deemed to be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, the Department is accredited by AICTE, UGC, and PCI, with its B.Pharm program recognized by the NBA.

Read the full department profile ＋

Supported by advanced infrastructure, modern laboratories, computing facilities, and a comprehensive library, it fosters high-quality research aligned with global standards. Collaborative initiatives with leading organizations and hospitals enhance its academic ecosystem, while robust placement drives ensure excellent career opportunities. The Department houses an IAEC-approved animal research facility under CPCSEA guidelines and a Human Ethics Committee compliant with ICMR standards. Additionally, it operates a generic medicine store under the PMBJP scheme, promoting access to affordable healthcare.

THE DIAITA PHILOSOPHY

A wider view of wellness.

In our pursuit of health and wellness, we need to expand our definition of “diet” beyond just the food we eat. It encompasses the media, cultural, spiritual, intellectual, emotional, and physical environments we engage with. When health outcomes fail to match the resources invested, it’s essential to challenge the systems that promote unhealthy lifestyles, including the industrial food complex, misinformation, and inactivity.

To drive meaningful change, we must adopt a holistic approach that goes beyond traditional dietary habits, cultivating environments that promote overall well-being. Health and wellness practitioners have a responsibility to encourage mindful choices—whether related to food, physical activity, policy, or lifestyle—and recognize that small, collective efforts can lead to profound societal transformation.
